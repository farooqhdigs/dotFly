## 💻 What Does dotFly Do?

Imagine a map of every single nerve cell (neuron) in a fruit fly's brain, and how those cells talk to each other. That's what dotFly works with. It takes that map—called a connectome—and brings it to life.

Instead of just showing you a static picture, dotFly lets you see the brain's activity happen in real time. You can watch as signals travel between neurons, just like they would in a living fly.

This is not a toy. It's the same kind of simulation that scientists use to understand how brains work. But we've made it easy enough for anyone to explore.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧪 Real Neuroscience Data

dotFly uses actual connectome data from two major research projects: MaleCNS and FlyWire. These are real maps of fruit fly brains that scientists have spent years building. You're not looking at made-up data—you're looking at the real thing.

### ⚡ Fast and Smooth

Because dotFly is built with .NET and uses special SIMD technology, it runs surprisingly fast even on regular computers. The simulation doesn't lag or freeze, so you can explore freely.

### 🎮 Built with Godot

The visual part of dotFly uses the Godot engine, which is known for making smooth, interactive 3D graphics. This means you get a beautiful, responsive view of the brain that's easy to navigate.

### 🪶 Lightweight

The whole program is small and efficient. It won't eat up your computer's memory or slow down your other work.

## 🖱️ How to Use dotFly

When you first open dotFly, you'll see a 3D view of the fly brain. It might look like a colorful tangle of lines and dots—that's normal!

Here's what you can do:

- **Rotate the view:** Click and drag with your mouse to spin the brain around.
- **Zoom in and out:** Use your mouse wheel to get closer or pull back.
- **Watch activity:** The simulation runs automatically. You'll see pulses of light or color moving along the neurons—that's the brain "firing."

Take your time. Explore different areas. There's no right or wrong way to look at it. You're seeing something that few people have ever seen before.

## 📚 Understanding the Science

If you're not a scientist, some of the terms might be confusing. Here's a quick translation:

- **Connectome:** A complete map of all the connections between neurons in a brain.
- **Neuron:** A nerve cell that sends and receives signals.
- **Spiking model:** A simulation where each neuron "fires" or sends a signal based on mathematical rules.
- **MaleCNS:** A dataset of the male fruit fly's central nervous system.
- **FlyWire:** A detailed map of the fruit fly brain, built by a research collaboration.

You don't need to memorize any of this. Just know that dotFly is showing you real science, not fantasy.
